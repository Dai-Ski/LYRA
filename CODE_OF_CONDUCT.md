# Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct applies to the **Lyra Community** and all spaces associated with the official repository at [LYRA](https://github.com/Dai-Ski/LYRA).

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Lyra project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or level of experience.

## Our Standards

* **Positive Behavior**: Empathy, constructive feedback, kind communication, and focusing on the community good.
* **Unacceptable Behavior**: Harassment, insults, personal attacks, exclusionary language, and unprofessional conduct.

## Enforcement & Reporting

**The Lyra Maintainers (governing body)** are responsible for enforcing these standards. Report any unacceptable behavior privately to:

* Email: [ado@daiski.dev](mailto:ado@daiski.dev)

We review and investigate all reports promptly and protect the reporter's privacy.
