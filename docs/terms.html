Lyra is engineered with strict privacy principles and does not collect, monitor, profile, or sell any personal information or telemetry.

The application operates entirely on your local machine, reading currently active song metadata directly from running media player applications such as Spotify and Apple Music through macOS system APIs.

When searching for synchronized lyrics, Lyra sends anonymous track metadata consisting solely of track title and artist name to public community databases.

No user accounts, unique device identifiers, IP addresses, playback histories, location coordinates, or keystrokes are ever gathered, stored, or transmitted to any centralized database or third-party analytic service.

All application preferences, appearance settings, and local cache files reside strictly on your local macOS filesystem and can be cleared or deleted by you at any time.
